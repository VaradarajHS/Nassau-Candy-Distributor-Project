## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes the sales and profitability performance of Nassau Candy Distributor using Python, data analytics, and an interactive Streamlit dashboard.

The objective is to identify key profitability drivers, evaluate product-level margins, understand cost structures, and generate actionable insights to support better business and pricing decisions.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The analysis focuses on answering the following business questions:

- Which products generate the highest revenue and gross profit?
- Which products have the strongest and weakest gross margins?
- How does cost of sales affect profitability?
- Which products require further monitoring?
- Where are potential opportunities for margin improvement?
- How can product profitability be improved through better pricing and cost management?

## 🔍 Analysis Approach

The project followed a structured business analytics approach:

### 1. Business Problem Definition

Defined the objective of understanding product-level profitability and margin performance to support better pricing, cost management, and product decisions.

### 2. Data Preparation

- Loaded the Nassau Candy Distributor sales dataset.
- Reviewed sales and cost-related fields.
- Cleaned and prepared the data for analysis.
- Validated the data for consistency.

### 3. KPI & Metric Development

Developed key profitability metrics including:

- Revenue
- Cost of Sales
- Gross Profit
- Gross Margin (%)
- Profit per Unit
- Cost as % of Sales

### 4. Product Profitability Analysis

Compared products based on:

- Revenue contribution
- Gross profit
- Gross margin
- Profit per unit
- Cost efficiency

### 5. Performance Segmentation

Evaluated products using profitability and margin indicators to identify:

- High-performing products
- Products requiring monitoring
- Potential margin improvement opportunities

### 6. Dashboard Development

Built an interactive Streamlit dashboard that allows users to explore profitability performance using:

- Date-range filters
- Division filters
- Product search
- Product-level profitability tables
- Interactive visualizations

### 7. Business Insights & Recommendations

Translated analytical findings into recommendations focused on:

- Improving margins
- Controlling costs
- Prioritizing profitable products
- Supporting data-driven business decisions

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights

The analysis enables decision-makers to identify:

- High-margin products that contribute strongly to profitability.
- Products with relatively high costs that may require optimization.
- Differences in profitability across products.
- Opportunities to prioritize high-performing products.
- Areas where pricing and cost management can improve profitability.
